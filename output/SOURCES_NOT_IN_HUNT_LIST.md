## TIER B: OFFICIAL / INDEX SERIES, FREE

### B1. Office of the Economic Adviser, WPI (official)
`https://eaindustry.nic.in/download_data_2223.asp`
Verified 12 Nov 2026. Monthly WPI, item-wise Excel files, free. Base 2022-23 series from Apr 2023; older series back to 1953. **Brass items are in the basket:** "Brass Metal/Sheet/Coils" and "Brass Tubes and Pipes".
Release: 14th of each month. Latest two months provisional.

### B2. IndiaInflation, WPI by item (free mirror)
`https://indiainflation.com/wpi/item/brass-metalsheetcoils`

| Item | Index (2022-23=100) | Month |
|---|---|---|
| Brass Metal/Sheet/Coils | 153.8 | Sep 2026 |
| Brass Tubes and Pipes | 172.3 | Sep 2026 |

Source: Office of the Economic Adviser (DPIIT) via MoSPI. Good for a monthly brass price index, not a ₹/kg spot.

### B3. RBI Database on Indian Economy, WPI
`https://dbie.rbihub.in/prices/wholesale-price-index`
Free, monthly, full commodity taxonomy, base years back to 1947. Good cross-check and long history.

### B4. World Bank Pink Sheet (copper and zinc legs)
`https://data.worldbank.org/data-catalog/commodity-price-data`
Free monthly PDF/Excel, 1960 to present. Copper Oct 2026: $14,508/mt (monthly avg). Zinc also present. Use to build the copper+zinc leg of a brass proxy.

### B5. FRED, IMF Global Price of Copper
`https://fred.stlouisfed.org/data/PCOPPUSDM`
Free monthly, USD/mt, 1992 to Sep 2026. Clean series for modelling.

### B6. WestMetall, LME daily prices
`https://westmetall.com/en/markdaten.php?action=table&field=LME_Cu_cash`
Free daily LME cash and 3-month tables for copper, zinc, lead, tin, nickel, aluminium. LME copper cash 11 Nov 2026: $13,875.50/mt.

### B7. Trading Economics, copper
`https://tradingeconomics.com/commodity/copper`
Free daily copper, historical data. Copper 11 Nov 2026: $6.28/lb.

## DATA-QUALITY WARNING: TWO PRICE CLUSTERS

Verified Indian brass scrap prices on 12 Nov 2026 split into two very different clusters:

| Cluster | Sources | ₹/kg | Likely basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| High | MTLEXS 875-995, AajBazar 580-1000, News18/PTI 875-978, Steel-Baba 720-950, ShreeMetalPrices 872-917, National Recycling 815, Adinath 810 | 720-1000 | Foundry buying board / wholesale, clean grades |
| Low | ScrapRates.in 375-425, GetScrapRate 402, ScrapXtrade 518 | 375-518 | Dealer-to-recycler baseline or mixed/household scrap |

Do not mix the two clusters in one series. Record the basis with every figure: foundry board, dealer pickup, wholesale baseline, or national average.

## BOTTOM LINE

The hunt list's Tier 1 (UN Comtrade) is still the best free historical series. But the free daily market is better than the hunt list claimed: **MTLEXS, AajBazar, News18/PTI, and Steel-Baba** give dated, citable ₹/kg by grade today, and **ScrapMonster and RecycleInMe are both live**, not 404. For a monthly official index, the **OEA WPI brass items** are free and already published. For paid intelligence with free previews, **Davis Index and SMM** are the two that actually cover Indian brass scrap weekly.
