Understanding Your Brass Business

Discovery questions for the owner, Castle Hardware — sent by [Your Name], [Roll No.], Department of Business Administration

Time needed: about 10 minutes · Reply any way you like: written, voice note, or a short call

1 · Why we are asking (the problem, honestly stated)

Brass prices keep moving, and every sudden jump eats into cost planning and export margins. This project will build you a simple weekly price guide — buy now or wait, what to quote, what to budget — but a good solution starts by deeply understanding how you actually buy, sell, and decide today. That is what these questions do. We have already studied your website — your brass builder-hardware lines, 40+ export countries, and Aligarh unit — so we will not ask about those. There are no wrong answers; your normal daily practice is exactly what we need to learn.

2 · How we will work with you (our course method)

1. ObserveLearn your buying & selling routine
2. DefinePin down the exact pain point
3. IdeateDesign the price guide with you
4. PrototypeFirst version on paper, fast
5. TestYour weekly feedback improves it

This follows the Design Thinking process from our syllabus (Units I–V): observation and personas, mind-mapping the cost drivers, cause-effect analysis, rapid prototyping, formative feedback, and action planning — applied to your real business.

WHO — the people

Q1. Who are your main suppliers (city/material), and who are your biggest customers, country by country?

WHAT — the material

Q2. What brass items do you buy — rods, strips, tubes? Which sizes and grades? (One supplier bill answers this fully.)

WHEN — timing and stock

Q3. How often do you place purchase orders — weekly, monthly, or as needed? What is one full load size?
Q4. If we alert you on a Monday morning that “prices may rise this week,” how fast can you actually place an order?

WHERE — markets and quotes

Q5. Which of your markets — US, Europe, Australia, UAE — order most often, and for how many days or weeks does each quotation stay valid?

HOW — price, payment, scrap and records

Q6. How is your buying price fixed — daily market rate, monthly average, or world price plus extra charges? (One recent quotation settles this.)
Q7. How do you handle scrap — melt it yourself, sell it, or buy only fresh material? Roughly what share is scrap?
Q8. Roughly how many kilos of brass do you buy in a month? A ballpark number is enough — no bills or documents needed, and only if you are comfortable sharing.

3 · What happens after you answer

We convert your answers into the first paper prototype of the price guide, bring it back to you for feedback, correct it, and only then build the working version. You review progress with us roughly once a week — short meetings, no long reports. Everything you share stays between us and is used only for this academic live project.

The two most valuable answers: your rough monthly quantity (Q8) and one recent supplier quotation (Q6). Everything else we can work around; those two we cannot.
