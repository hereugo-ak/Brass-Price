Model inputs, for whoever maintains this

The floor is produced by the backend engine and cannot be edited here. If a value below looks wrong, fix the feed in the Data tab — that is the only place inputs change.

What this is. Metal value from MCX copper and zinc × the measured brass ratio, plus transport, plus a cushion for the period the quote is held, plus margin.

What it is not. A forecast. Two independent metal routes agree to 0.42%, which is why the level can be trusted. Week-to-week direction cannot be: eight models were trained and all lost to a random walk. So this is a floor, not a prediction.
