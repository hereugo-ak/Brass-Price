Metal value comes from MCX copper and zinc. Ratio is the measured brass-to-metal ratio (bartan + honey + purja, n=16). The quote floor adds one standard deviation of the ratio, the metal-move cushion for the cover period, transport, and your margin.
How this number is built. Metal value from MCX copper and zinc × measured brass ratio + transport, then a cushion for the period the quote is held, then margin.

What it is not. Not a prediction. Two independent metal routes — LME×FX and MCX — agree to 0.42%, which is why the level can be trusted; the range is wide because prices are close to a random walk week to week. That is why this is a floor, not a forecast.
