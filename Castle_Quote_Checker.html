Model inputs, for whoever maintains this

Metal value = 0.615 × MCX copper + 0.385 × MCX zinc. Ratio is measured from eleven Indian boards (bartan, honey, purja; n=16). The floor adds one ratio standard deviation, the metal-move cushion for the cover period, transport and margin.

Weekly note

Send this at the start of the week

What this is. Metal value from MCX copper and zinc, multiplied by the measured brass ratio, plus transport, plus a cushion for the period the quote is held, plus margin.

What it is not. A forecast. Two independent metal routes — LME × FX and MCX — agree to 0.42%, which is why the level can be trusted. Week-to-week direction cannot be: eight models were trained and all lost to a random walk. So this is a floor, not a prediction.
